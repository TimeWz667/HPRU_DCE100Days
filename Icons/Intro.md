# Introduction

We will show you several choice activities about possible responses in a future pandemic. These scenarios are described in terms of:

-   The type of public health measures in place and how long they last

-   The expected impacts on:

    1.  Health (cases/deaths)

    2.  Education

    3.  The economy

-   Whether a vaccine is available by the end of the measures

## The public health measures and duration

To reduce infections and deaths before a vaccine is available, the government may introduce different packages of public health measures (for example, social distancing or a lockdown).

In the options you will be shown, these measures may be in place intermittently over a period of 3, 6, or 9 months. This means restrictions may be introduced, eased, and reintroduced during that time.

### Vaccines

Vaccines can now be developed more quickly than in the past. In each scenario, you will be told **whether a vaccine is expected to be available** when the public health measures end.

**If a vaccine becomes available, it may help society return to normal more quickly by providing another way to protect people from the disease**.

You should assume the vaccine would be:

-   Approved by the NHS as safe and effective. This means: 
    -   Common side effects are mild and serious side effects are very rare 
    -   The vaccine greatly reduces the risk of severe illness and death 
-   Free of out-of-pocket charge
-   Available to everyone, but rolled out in phases  

You would not be required to get the vaccine, but it is strongly recommended to help protect yourself and others.
